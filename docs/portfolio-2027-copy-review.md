# Case-study copy review — 26 September 2026

The owner requested a review of every project using Gusto and Vizcom as references: descriptive, specific copy without overstating the result. Scope includes the 12 current cases, nine archived projects, project metadata, card summaries, media descriptions and collaborator previews. The archives retain their direct URLs and noindex/nofollow policy.

## Editorial approach

- Describe the task and the visible work in plain language. Use first person for the artist’s contribution and distinguish collaborators’ roles.
- Give each section a purpose: the brief, the decisions, the contribution and the delivered result. Avoid repeated service lists, generic praise and claims of audience or business impact without evidence.
- Describe deliverables by what they are. Remove redundant production counts, resolutions and turnaround claims from the sales copy.
- Keep Fun introductions personal without implying that the projects were unpaid or self-initiated.
- Retain supported historical figures with attribution and their limits. Do not turn campaign totals into claims about one contributor.
